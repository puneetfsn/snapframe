# SnapFrame — Free Screenshot Beautifier

**Live app:** https://puneetfsn.github.io/snapframe/

SnapFrame is a free, mobile-first screenshot beautifier. Edit images in your browser — no account, no ads, no upload to a server.

## What it does

- Beautify one screenshot (frames, gradients, wood/mountain scenes)
- Stitch 2–4 scrolling screenshots into one tall image
- Batch-style several screenshots the same way
- Crop, captions, privacy cover (hide bank/OTP/names)
- Add your own logo or photo (5% / 10% / 25%, drag to move)
- Export PNG, copy, or share
- Install as a PWA on Android / iPhone

## Who it is for

People who take screenshots on a **phone** and want them ready for Instagram, X, LinkedIn, or app-store posts — without opening a laptop.
